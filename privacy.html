Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Invoice & Rate Kit (the “site”), including the free invoice generator and the free rate calculator.

1. The short version

Nothing you type into the invoice generator or the rate calculator is sent to us or to anyone else. Both tools run entirely in your browser. Your invoice drafts and calculator values are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, and you can delete them at any time.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for an account, an email address or a password to use the tools.
  • We do not receive the names, addresses, tax IDs, bank details, rates or amounts you enter.
  • We do not receive the logos you upload. The image is read by your browser and stored locally; it is never transmitted.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

The tools use your browser's local storage — not cookies — to remember your work between visits:

  • your invoice draft, including business details, client details, line items and an uploaded logo (kept as an image in local storage);
  • your calculator inputs and currency preference;
  • a counter used to suggest the next invoice number.

This data stays on your device, is readable only by this site in your browser, and is removed when you clear your browser's site data or use the “Reset fields” button. Because it is local, it is not backed up by us: if you clear your browser, the draft is gone. Save your PDF before you close the tab.

4. Hosting and server logs

The site is served as a static website by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That processing is described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

5. Advertising

Some pages display advertising served by third parties, currently Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Where required by law, a consent message is shown before advertising cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time through that message.

Advertising cookies are never placed on the invoice sheet, and the invoice you print or save as PDF contains no advertising.

6. Analytics

If aggregate, privacy-friendly traffic measurement is used, it records page views and country-level location only — never the contents of a form. No analytics code reads your invoice fields.

7. The Pro Freelancer Kit (paid download)

Payments and digital delivery for the paid template bundle are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy processes your email address, payment details and billing country under its own privacy policy; we receive the order details needed to provide the download and to answer support requests. We do not see or store your card number.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold the content of your invoices or calculator inputs, requests to access or delete that data are met by clearing your own browser storage. If you are in the EEA, the UK, California or another jurisdiction with data-protection rights, you may still contact us about any information we do hold (for example a purchase record) through the site's issue tracker, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

9. Children

The site is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at the project's issue tracker.
