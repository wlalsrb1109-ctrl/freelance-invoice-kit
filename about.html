About

A tool that does one job and costs nothing

Invoice & Rate Kit exists because invoicing a client should not require an account, a trial, a watermark or a credit card — and because most freelancers are quietly charging the wrong number.

What this is

Two free tools that run entirely in your browser:

  • A free invoice generator — live A4 or US Letter preview, tax, discount, deposit and balance, your logo, and a one-click PDF. It also writes quotes, pro forma invoices, receipts and credit notes.
  • A freelance rate calculator — works backwards from your desired take-home pay through expenses, tax, a slow-month buffer and the hours you can truly bill, and includes a project quote builder and a tax set-aside estimator.

There is no server behind the invoice generator. The page is a static file; the invoice you type exists only in your browser's local storage on your device. That is a deliberate design choice, not a limitation: client names, rates and bank details are the kind of data that should not need a privacy policy to be safe.

How it is funded

Advertising (Google AdSense) on the content pages, and an optional paid bundle of Excel, Word and Notion templates called the Pro Freelancer Kit at $14 once. The free tools are not a trial and do not expire — the paid kit is for people who want to track invoices across a year, not for people who need to send one today.

How it is built

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served from GitHub Pages, with no build step and no third-party framework. The source is public: wlalsrb1109-ctrl/freelance-invoice-kit. If something is wrong, an issue there is the fastest way to reach us — and a pull request is even better.

Contact

Use the repository's issue tracker for bug reports, feature requests, licence questions and refunds. It is public, so please do not paste client data or personal details into an issue.
