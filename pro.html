Questions before you buy

Do I need Excel?

No. The workbook opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice. If you use Google Sheets, upload the .xlsx to Drive and it converts. The Notion template covers the same ground if you would rather not use a spreadsheet.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment of $14. The download link stays valid and includes future updates to the same kit.

Will the free tools stay free?

Yes. The invoice generator and the rate calculator are free, unlimited, with no watermark and no account. The Pro kit is the optional extra for tracking and pricing — not a paywall on the tools.

What if it doesn’t fit how I work?

Ask for a refund within 14 days and you get it, no questions. Digital downloads are otherwise final, so please look at the free sample first.

Can I use it for my agency or my clients?

The licence covers your own freelance business. If you want to use it inside a team or hand it to clients, write to us through the GitHub repository and we will sort out a team licence.
